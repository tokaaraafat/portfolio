01 · 2026 · SQL and Python

City Bike-Share Analysis

Problem
When do people ride Capital Bikeshare bikes, where and on what, and how does the weather change it?
Key findings
  • Ridership peaks at 17:00, with a second peak at 08:00.
  • Members make 61% of rides, and casual trips are 2.1x longer.
  • Electric bikes grew from about 15% of trips (2020 to 2022) to 51% in 2024.
  • Rain cuts ridership by 22% from June to September.
