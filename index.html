01 · 2025 · Excel

Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard

Which categories, regions and cities drive sales, and how do returns and discounts affect net sales?

Data
Retail sales data across four tables: about 10,000 order lines, returns, sales reps and shipping costs.
Cleaning and transformation
Combined the tables in Power Query into one data model and derived COGS, discount value and delivery-duration fields.
Analysis and dashboard
KPI dashboard (total and net sales, returns, discounts, COGS, customers, orders) built with 20 pivot tables, 14 charts and slicers for city, sales rep, category and return status.
Insight
From 2014 to 2017 revenue grew 51%. Technology was the top category at 36% of sales, with California and New York City as the top state and city. Sales are concentrated in a few categories and locations, which makes them the first place to look when planning stock and targets.

02 · 2026 · Python

Financial Performance Visualizer and Analytics Dashboard

How do a company’s stock price and quarterly revenue move together, and how volatile is each stock?

Data
Daily stock prices from yfinance and quarterly revenue tables scraped with BeautifulSoup, for Tesla and GameStop.
Cleaning and transformation
Merged the revenue tables with the daily prices in Pandas into one dataset.
Analysis and dashboard
Streamlit dashboard with dual-axis Plotly charts, a 50-day moving average and metrics for current price, annualized volatility and latest quarterly revenue.
Insight
When captured (Oct 2026), GameStop’s annualized volatility was 82.5% versus 57.3% for Tesla, whose latest quarterly revenue was $21,454M. Price risk differed widely between the two, so revenue and price are best read side by side.

03 · 2026 · Excel

Bike Sales Customer Analysis Dashboard

Which customers buy bikes, and what separates buyers from non-buyers?

Data
A customer dataset of 1,000 records.
Cleaning and transformation
Cleaned the records and engineered age brackets.
Analysis and dashboard
Interactive Excel dashboard with slicers for marital status, region and education, analysing purchases by age, income, gender and commute distance.
Insight
Middle-aged customers bought at 55% versus 31 to 36% for other age groups, and purchase rates fell from about 55% under 1 mile to about 30% beyond 10 miles. Age bracket and commute distance are useful starting points for segmenting customers.
